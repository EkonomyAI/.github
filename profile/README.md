# Ekonomy AI

**Helping independent restaurant operators turn everyday know-how into repeatable operations.**

Ekonomy AI is the company behind **ProvisionOS**. We’re building practical software that helps owners make their judgment easier to share across managers, locations, and shifts—starting with coaching and training.

## What we’re building

Restaurants run on knowledge that rarely makes it into a manual: how an owner handles an exception, what a manager should watch for, and what good service looks like on a busy shift. ProvisionOS is being built to make that knowledge easier to capture, find, and put to work.

Our initial focus:

- **Capture operational knowledge:** preserve owner-specific standards and recurring exceptions as reusable context.
- **Support managers and staff:** bring that context into everyday coaching and training.
- **Keep teams consistent:** help carry lessons across shifts and locations instead of repeatedly starting from scratch.

We’re starting with focused restaurant workflows, not a one-size-fits-all AI platform. The goal is less repeated owner intervention and more consistent execution by the team.
